Here’s a **ready-to-deliver “Tell me about yourself” answer**  
following Charles Webb’s **BRAVO framework** (Begin with gratitude – Role – Academic/Path – Vision – Out-of-office).  
This version runs about **2 minutes** when spoken at a natural pace.

---

### **B – Begin with gratitude**

“Thank you so much for the opportunity to speak with you today.  
I’m really excited about this role and the chance to contribute to AWS.”

---

### **R – Current Role**

“Since 2022 I’ve been the **Lead Data & AI Consultant at Mantel Group in Australia**,  
where I guide enterprise clients on cloud architecture, machine learning, and generative AI.  
During these three years I’ve taken on progressively senior positions—from technical leadership and knowledge transfer, to enterprise data-platform architecture, and most recently **AI agent architecture**—delivering large-scale transformations and measurable business outcomes.”

---

### **A – Academic & Career Path**

“I began my career after completing a **Bachelor’s in Electronic Engineering** in 2012.  
Early on I worked as a **research scholar in Malaysia**, then as a software engineer and data scientist across several organisations including **AirAsia, TMR&D, and Juris Technologies**.  
By 2019, when I joined **REA Group**, I was leading teams and delivering end-to-end data and AI projects.  
Along the way I earned a **Master’s in Engineering Science focused on Artificial Intelligence**, and I’m now completing a **PhD in generative statistics and IoT sensors**.  
These experiences gave me both a deep technical foundation and a strong background in applied machine learning and cloud-native architecture.”

---

### **V – Vision**

“Looking ahead, my goal is to be part of a company that **pushes the frontier of data analytics and generative AI at global scale**.  
AWS is the ideal environment for me to keep learning, collaborate with world-class engineers, and help customers innovate with cloud-first, AI-driven solutions that improve millions of lives.”

---

### **O – Out of Office (human touch)**

“Outside work, I live in Perth with my wife **Sanjida** and our two children, **Sahiem** and **Muntaha**, aged five and four.  
Weekends are family time—we love exploring Western Australia’s parks and coastlines and introducing the kids to Australia’s wildlife.”

---

✅ **Tips for delivery**

- Aim for **~2 minutes total**.
    
- Smile when mentioning family; it adds warmth and memorability.
    
- If pressed for time, you can shorten the academic path to:  
    _“Earlier in my career I worked as a researcher and data scientist across Malaysia and Australia, before moving into leadership at RIA Group.”_
    

---

This answer:

- Flows naturally with **BRAVO**.
    
- Covers **key leadership signals** Amazon values (Customer Obsession, Learn & Be Curious, Invent & Simplify).
    
- Ends with a **personal touch** that helps interviewers connect with you as a colleague.
