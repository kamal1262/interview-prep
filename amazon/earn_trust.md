Here’s a **long-term, metric-rich STAR-HR version** of your **Earn Trust** story  
that highlights a **5-year financial impact** Amazon interviewers will appreciate.

---

## Headline

In 2024, while leading a search-platform modernization for Australia Post client (~1 million monthly searches), I rebuilt trust with a skeptical architecture forum and delivered a design that **reduces operating costs by over 85 %—from $135 K to under $20 K per month—saving more than $6.5 million over five years**, all while enabling the client’s in-house team to manage the platform independently.  
Let me explain this in more detail.

---

## S – Situation

Early 2024, I was the Lead Data & AI Consultant for a client wanting to uplift their internal Q&A search system.  
The service processed **~1 million queries per month** and cost roughly **$135 K/month (~$1.6 M/year)** in cloud operations.  
Our solution required approval from the client’s architecture forum.  
Although the project started smoothly, within two weeks I noticed slower email responses and reduced engagement—indicators that stakeholder confidence in our proposed high-level design might be weakening.

---

## T – Task

I needed to **regain stakeholder trust and alignment**, obtain timely architectural approval, and ensure the final design was both **financially sustainable for at least five years** and **fully manageable by the client’s small internal team**.

---

## A – Action

- **Diagnosed the trust issue.** I treated the drop in responsiveness as a red flag and scheduled a face-to-face meeting with the project manager and key stakeholder.
    
- **Unearthed root causes.** Through open questions, I learned the architecture forum required a **detailed, component-level diagram** and explicit cost projections—not just a high-level view.
    
- **Delivered fast, data-driven value.** Within 24 hours I produced the detailed design, modelled long-term costs, and validated scalability to **over 1 million searches/month**.
    
- **Enhanced transparency.** I instituted weekly updates and architecture previews, so stakeholders could see progress continuously and stay confident in the process.
    

---

## R – Result

- The architecture forum **approved both designs within two days**.
    
- The optimized design **cut monthly costs from $135 K to under $20 K**—a **savings of roughly $1.3 M per year**.
    
- Projected over five years, the solution delivers **$6.5 M+ in total savings**.
    
- The design ensured **full in-house manageability**, eliminating ongoing dependency on external vendors.
    
- Stakeholder engagement rebounded immediately, allowing the project to stay on schedule for launch.
    

---

## H – Reflection

This experience proved that **trust depends on transparency, measurable financial impact, and understanding each stakeholder’s preferred level of detail and communication**.  
I now start every project by:

- Requesting sample approved diagrams to set the right depth of documentation.
    
- Agreeing on update cadence and cost-saving goals from day one.
    
- Watching for non-verbal signals—like declining responsiveness—that may signal misalignment.
    

These practices help me **earn trust quickly and avoid costly rework**, ensuring long-term value in the multi-million-dollar range.

---

- **Five-year savings:** **$6.5 M+**.
    
- **Time to regain trust and sign-off:** **2 days**.
    

This 5-year framing shows **strategic, durable business impact**, perfect for Amazon’s

# Application to other principals 

Your **Earn Trust** story can flex to several other **Amazon Leadership Principles** because it demonstrates multiple behaviors Amazon values.  
Here’s how it maps, with angles you can emphasize for each.

---

### 1️⃣ **Customer Obsession**

_Why it fits:_

- You detected a drop in engagement and immediately focused on **understanding the client’s needs** (detailed architecture diagram, more frequent updates).
    
- You prioritised their confidence and long-term maintainability over your own convenience.
    

_How to angle the story:_

> “I constantly put the customer first—recognising unspoken concerns and reshaping my delivery style to meet their expectations and build a sustainable solution.”

---

### 2️⃣ **Dive Deep**

_Why it fits:_

- You investigated **why** communication was slowing instead of assuming it was personal.
    
- You drilled into architectural standards and reference diagrams to close gaps.
    

_How to angle the story:_

> “Rather than accept surface signals, I dug into the root cause of the hesitation and verified the exact level of technical detail required.”

---

### 3️⃣ **Are Right, A Lot**

_Why it fits:_

- You made a data-driven decision to adjust the architecture documentation quickly.
    
- Your technical judgment was validated when both high-level and detailed designs were approved without rework.
    

_How to angle the story:_

> “By testing assumptions and seeking evidence, I ensured the final solution was both technically sound and fully aligned with stakeholder standards.”

---

### 4️⃣ **Invent and Simplify**

_Why it fits:_

- You streamlined the process by proposing a **parallel approach** (high-level + detailed diagram) instead of waiting for long review cycles.
    
- You simplified communication, creating a predictable update rhythm.
    

_How to angle the story:_

> “I simplified how we shared progress and artifacts, making it easy for a diverse audience to review and approve quickly.”

---

### 5️⃣ **Bias for Action**

_Why it fits:_

- You didn’t wait for formal escalation—you set up an immediate meeting and produced a detailed diagram within 24 hours.
    
- Quick action prevented project delays.
    

_How to angle the story:_

> “When I sensed a potential stall, I acted within hours to clarify expectations and deliver what was needed to move forward.”

---

### 6️⃣ **Deliver Results**

_Why it fits:_

- You kept the project on schedule and preserved client confidence.
    
- The outcome was measurable: on-time approval and uninterrupted delivery.
    

_How to angle the story:_

> “Despite early misalignment, I kept the project on track and achieved on-time architectural sign-off.”

---

## How to Use This

- **Map one story to multiple LPs** by slightly adjusting the introduction and Action/Result focus.
    
- For example, for _Dive Deep_ highlight root-cause analysis; for _Bias for Action_ emphasise speed and decisive follow-up; for _Customer Obsession_ stress proactive relationship-building.
    

> **Tip:** Keep the same core Situation/Task, but tweak the **Headline and Action emphasis** for each principle so it feels fresh and targeted.
