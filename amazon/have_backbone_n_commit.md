## Headline

In late 2024 I challenged my team’s push for a custom video-streaming architecture and advocated instead for an AWS-proven IoT Greengrass solution, ensuring the client received a scalable, maintainable product ready for the 2026 Olympic rollout.  
Let me explain this in more detail.

---

## S – Situation

I was the Lead Data & AI Consultant at Mantle Group, working on a high-visibility project for Swimming Australia.  
The goal was to build an IoT-enabled analytics platform that captured and streamed swimming performance data from 20–25 venues with variable internet connectivity.  
The client had only a three-person tech team, yet the solution had to be production-ready by early 2026 to support Olympic competition coverage.

---

## T – Task

During discovery, some team members proposed discarding AWS IoT Greengrass running on Nvidia Jetson devices—already an established, battle-tested stack—and instead building a custom streaming framework from scratch.  
While attractive from an engineering perspective, I believed this would create serious risks: higher cost, longer development, and a maintenance burden the client could not sustain.  
My responsibility was to ensure the final architecture met the tight eight-week engagement timeline and would be sustainable for Swimming Australia long term.

---

## A – Action

I led a structured technical and customer-impact assessment.

- I gathered metrics on cost, scalability, and failure points for both the custom proposal and the Greengrass-based design.
    
- In team meetings, I posed the question **“Better for whom?”**, highlighting that the custom plan offered many benefits for us but few for the client’s small in-house team.
    
- To keep discussions objective, I proposed running a parallel proof-of-concept: one track with the custom code and one with the AWS-standard approach.
    
- I facilitated workshops with the client to ensure their needs and long-term support capacity were front and center.
    
- Throughout, I kept communication transparent and collaborative, so even those who preferred the custom route felt heard and remained engaged.
    

---

## R – Result

Within eight weeks we delivered a production-ready architecture built on AWS IoT Greengrass.  
The custom track failed to meet performance and scaling requirements (e.g., MQTT protocol complexity), validating our data-driven decision.  
The client adopted the Greengrass solution, praising its reliability and maintainability.  
This choice reduced estimated operating costs by **~25 %**, avoided future re-engineering, and kept the project on track for the 2026 Olympic deadline.

---

## H – Reflection

This experience reinforced for me that **customer obsession outweighs engineering ego**.  
I learned that respectful pushback—framed around measurable client impact and supported by quick POCs—builds trust and accelerates consensus.  
Next time, I will start parallel prototypes even earlier to de-risk decisions sooner, but I will continue to ask the simple, powerful question: _“Better for whom?”_
