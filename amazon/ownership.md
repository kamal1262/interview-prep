## Why It Fits Ownership

Amazon defines **Ownership** as: _“Leaders act on behalf of the entire company, beyond just their own team. They think long term and don’t say ‘that’s not my job.’”_

Your REA recommender story demonstrates that by:

- **Taking end-to-end responsibility** for an unproven, high-impact initiative—from spotting the opportunity to production launch.
    
- **Owning risk and results**: you designed success/failure metrics and were ready to roll back if the model didn’t deliver.
    
- **Driving cross-team collaboration** to product and data science groups for a business-critical uplift, not just a technical experiment.
    
- **Thinking long term** by creating a reusable reference design that influenced other products.

## Ownership-Focused Reframe (STAR-HR)

### **H – Headline**

“I took full ownership of designing and deploying a next-generation real-time property recommendation engine at REA Group—personally guiding it from research idea to production, delivering a **27-point absolute uplift in click-through (7 % → 32 %)** and setting a reusable design pattern for future products.”

---

### **S – Situation**

In 2023 at REA Group, our property site served millions of users but our legacy recommender achieved only ~7 % click-through.  
No one was specifically tasked to overhaul it, but I recognised the competitive risk and opportunity presented by new transformer-based sequence models emerging in research.

---

### **T – Task**

Go beyond my formal role to **own the entire lifecycle** of a radical technology shift—  
from hypothesis and R&D to production deployment—delivering measurable business impact quickly and safely.

---

### **A – Action**

- Independently researched transformer models and hypothesised they could model property-search sequences like natural language.
    
- Secured buy-in from product management and data science, even though this work was outside my original remit.
    
- Built a proof of concept and a real-time streaming pipeline on AWS, defined clear success metrics (≥20 pp CTR uplift) and rollback criteria.
    
- Drove cross-team collaboration, daily stand-ups, and risk management, ensuring accountability for technical and business outcomes.
    

---

### **R – Result**

- Production deployment **within months** (faster than a normal product cycle).
    
- **Click-through improved from ~7 % to ~32 %**, a **27-point absolute increase**.
    
- Delivered **multi-million-dollar annual revenue upside** and a **reference design** that other REA products now follow.
    

---

### **Reflection (HR)**

“This project showed me that true ownership means _seeing the need, not waiting for an assignment_, and staying accountable from concept to long-term operational success.  
I now habitually define up-front metrics, take calculated risks, and build reusable solutions that benefit the wider organisation.”
