## Question

Tell me a time when you stayed up to date with emerging technologies and applied them to your work

---

### 🏆 Leadership Principles This Story Demonstrates

- **Learn and Be Curious** – you proactively explored a brand-new algorithmic approach inspired by NLP/transformer research.
    
- **Invent and Simplify** – you applied that concept to a different domain to create a simpler, more effective recommendation engine.
    
- **Are Right, A Lot** – you made a calculated bet on an unproven method and delivered strong, measurable business results.
    
- **Customer Obsession** – improved personalization directly increased customer engagement.
    

---

### **H – Headline (direct, 1–2 sentences)**

“One example is when I led the design of a **next-generation real-time property recommendation engine** at REA Group.  
I brought in a brand-new transformer-style sequence model—then considered ‘cutting-edge NLP’—and adapted it to e-commerce property data, boosting click-through rate by about **27 percentage points**.”

---

### **S – Situation**

- **Where/When:** At RIA Group (a large property-tech company), we needed to increase relevance of property recommendations on our high-traffic website.
    
- **Business context:** Customers include investors, renters, and first-home buyers with different price ranges and location preferences. Existing recommender algorithms (market-basket, collaborative filtering) produced only ~7 % click-through.
    
- **Trigger:** Around that time, new transformer-based sequence models (similar to those underpinning GPT) were emerging in academic and open-source communities.
    

---

### **T – Task**

- Explore and adopt a more modern algorithm to capture **sequential patterns in user journeys**—for example, how search and view behaviour evolves as buyers move toward purchase.
    
- Deliver measurable uplift in personalization within a short commercial window, with only a **2-3 person R&D team** and no guarantee of success.
    

---

### **A – Action**

- **Continuous learning:** Regularly read research papers, AWS and industry blogs; noticed a post on adapting language-model sequence prediction for recommendation systems.
    
- **Technical strategy:** Hypothesised that **property-search behaviour forms a sequence akin to words in a sentence**. Decided to adapt a transformer-style model to learn these patterns.
    
- **Design & implementation:**
    
    - Created a proof of concept combining user-event sequence embeddings with real-time feature updates.
        
    - Built a streaming pipeline to capture events and feed them to the model using Python and Spark on AWS.
        
    - Partnered with product managers to ensure business relevance and with data scientists to validate model accuracy.
        
- **Risk management:** Took ownership of potential failure; ran experiments quickly and set clear “go/no-go” metrics so that, if results lagged, we could revert to the legacy model.
    

---

### **R – Result**

- Deployed the new recommender into production **within a few months**, well ahead of a typical product cycle.
    
- **Click-through rate jumped from ~7 % to ~32 %** on recommended properties—a **27 percentage-point absolute uplift**.
    
- Conversion and engagement metrics improved accordingly, giving customers faster access to properties matching their true preferences.
    
- The project became a reference design for other RIA products.
    

---

### **Reflection (HR)**

“This experience reinforced that **staying curious and experimenting early with emerging tech can create tangible customer and business impact**.  
By translating state-of-the-art NLP concepts into property recommendations and taking a calculated risk, we delivered a step-change in engagement and set a new internal benchmark for innovation.”
