
### Delivery & Communication

- **Watch/listen/read your recordings 24 hrs later** to review objectively, not emotionally.
    
- **Identify filler words** (“um”, “like”) and reduce them.
    
- **Add vocal rhythm and emphasis** (e.g., “First…, second…”) instead of a monotone.
    
- **Use hand gestures** to create natural emphasis and energy.
    
- **Maintain a pleasant, engaged facial expression**—avoid a “resting bothered face”.
    
- **Match tone to content** so excitement sounds genuine, not flat or sarcastic.
    
- **Slow slightly** to make your STAR structure clear and easy to follow.
    
- **Remember 55/38/7 rule**: 55 % non-verbal, 38 % tone, 7 % words.
    

### Structure & Storytelling

- **Open with a bold headline statement** that shows how you help the customer, not ego.
    
- **Use the STAR-HR format** (Headline, Situation, Task, Action, Result, Reflection).
    
- **Keep each story organized and concise** so interviewers can take notes easily.
    
- **Map each story to multiple Amazon Leadership Principles**—one story can support several principles (e.g., Customer Obsession, Invent & Simplify, Are Right a Lot).
    
- **Prepare at least 3 stories per principle** to avoid repeating the same example.
    

### Mindset & Framing

- **Focus on customer benefit**—keep asking “Better for whom?” to stay customer-centric.
    
- **Show excitement about impact** and how your skills help the company and end users.
    
- **Highlight collaboration and trust-building** when you push back or influence decisions.
    
- **Present achievements as value for them, not self-promotion**.
    

### Practical Steps

- **Curate your background for video interviews**; let it reflect professionalism and personality.
    
- **Use the BRAVO model** (Begin with gratitude, Role, Academic/pathway, Vision, Outside interests) for “Tell me about yourself”.
    
- **Prepare 5–6 core stories** and tag them with multiple principles for flexible reuse.
